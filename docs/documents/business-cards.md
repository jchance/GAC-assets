# Business Cards

Every Griffin Arts Council board member gets the same card. The front has the logo, the office address, and your name, title, phone, and email. There are two navy backs, each with a QR code:

- **Membership** links to [griffinarts.org/membership](https://griffinarts.org/membership/). It's the one to hand out in most conversations.
- **Sponsor** links to [griffinarts.org/become-a-sponsor](https://griffinarts.org/become-a-sponsor/), for local business owners.

MOO prints both backs in one order at no extra cost, so you can get a mix.

![The front of a sample Griffin Arts Council business card and both backs](/images/business-cards/business-card-preview.png){: style="border: 1px solid #d1d5db; box-shadow: 0 1px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08);"}

## Getting Your Cards

1. Open the [card preview](/business-cards/card.html) and type your name, title, email, and phone. The card updates as you type, so you can see how it'll look.
2. Send those details to [hello@griffinarts.org](mailto:hello@griffinarts.org). We'll make your print files and send them back with a proof.
3. Check the proof, then order from MOO (the steps are below).

Leave the phone blank if you'd rather not list one. That line drops off the card.

## Ordering from MOO

We print on MOO's US standard business cards (3.5 × 2 in).

1. On moo.com, start a US standard business card order and choose to upload your own design.
2. Upload your front PDF as the front. For the back, upload both back PDFs and set how many of each you want. Upload each file on its own. MOO doesn't take a single file with more than one side.
3. Look over MOO's preview. The edges of the background will look cut off, and that's expected. It's the bleed.
4. Pick your paper and quantity, then check out.

## Print Specs

These match MOO's artwork guidelines for US standard cards.

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Trimmed size | 3.5 × 2 in |
| File size with bleed | 3.66 × 2.16 in (0.08 in bleed on each side) |
| Safe area | Text and logo stay 0.1875 in inside the trim. MOO asks for 0.08 in. |
| Files | One PDF per side, PDF/X-1a, no crop marks |
| Color | CMYK, Coated GRACoL 2006 |
| Text | Rucksack, turned into outlines |
| Logo | Horizontal logo, 1.4 in wide |
| Colors | Deep Navy, Teal, Coral, and Gold from the [brand palette]({% link docs/logos/usage-guidelines.md %}#2-brand-color-palette) |

The steps for making cards and updating the design are on the [Business Cards page in the repository wiki](https://github.com/jchance/GAC-assets/wiki/Business-Cards).
